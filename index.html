CHEST PRESS

Build a stronger chest with proper form.
HOW TO USE THE CHEST PRESS◷ 0:30
STEP-BY-STEP GUIDE
1 / 3
1
Adjust the seat

Set the seat so the handles are level with the middle of your chest. Keep your back flat against the pad.

ⓘ Why it mattersThe right seat height helps you target the chest properly and keeps your shoulders in a safe position.
PERFORM THE MOVEMENT
1 / 3
1
Press forward

Press the handles forward smoothly without locking your elbows.

ⓘ Key cueKeep your wrists neutral and focus on a smooth, controlled movement.

MUSCLES WORKED

The chest press primarily targets your chest, with additional activation in your triceps and front shoulders.

Primary: Pectoralis Major (Chest)

Main muscle worked. Helps produce the main pushing force.

Secondary: Triceps Brachii

Assists the press by extending your elbows.

Secondary: Anterior Deltoid

Assists as your upper arm moves forward.

QUICK TIPS

✓ Set the seat so the handles start around mid-chest.
✓ Keep your back against the pad and feet planted.
✓ Keep your wrists neutral.
✓ Use a weight you can control through the full movement.
✓ Avoid bouncing or slamming the weight stack.

SAFETY

Use a controllable weight and move smoothly. Keep your shoulders against the pad, avoid forcefully locking your elbows, and stop if you experience pain. Ask gym staff for assistance if you are unsure about the setup.
