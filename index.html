CHEST PRESS

Build a stronger chest with proper form.
HOW TO USE THE CHEST PRESS
▶

SETUP

1
Adjust the seat

Set the seat so the handles sit around mid-chest height.

2
Position yourself

Sit with your back against the pad and your feet flat on the floor.

3
Grip the handles

Keep your wrists straight and use a comfortable grip.

PERFORM

1
Push forward

Press the handles forward smoothly without locking your elbows hard.

2
Return slowly

Bring the handles back under control and repeat.

MUSCLES WORKED

The chest press primarily targets your chest, with additional activation in your triceps and front shoulders.

Primary: Pectoralis Major (Chest)

Main muscle worked. The chest press strengthens and builds your pectoralis major, helping with upper-body strength and pushing movements.

Secondary: Triceps Brachii (Triceps)

Assists the pressing movement by extending the elbows as you push the handles forward.

Secondary: Anterior Deltoid (Front Shoulders)

Assists the chest during the pressing movement and helps move the upper arm forward.

QUICK TIPS

✓ Don't set the seat too high or too low.
✓ Keep your back against the pad.
✓ Avoid flaring your elbows too far out.
✓ Choose a weight you can control.
✓ Don't bounce or slam the weight stack.

SAFETY

Use a controllable weight and move smoothly. If you are unsure about the setup or experience pain, stop and ask gym staff for assistance.
